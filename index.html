Who I am

Where data meets the P&L

I specialize in data analysis, process optimization, and stakeholder management — but what I actually spend my time doing is building the tools that make those things possible. Pricing engines, PIM pipelines, ERP integrations: systems that quietly do the routine work so people can focus on the decisions that matter.

My approach is rooted in Design Thinking — understand what the user actually needs, generate ideas, prototype fast, test with the people who'll use it. As my own project manager on most of what I build, I've learned to work closely with stakeholders across Commercial, Sales, and B2B, translating complex datasets and SQL insights into tools non-technical teams can trust.

I'm continually pulled toward new tools and technologies — most of what I know about automation and modern tooling, I taught myself, and I still spend my free time doing exactly that.
